About Me

I'm an MSc student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics with over eight years of experience in the oil and gas industry. I combine domain knowledge with data science to explore how complex operational data can be turned into actionable insights that support better decision-making.

I am currently building my skills in machine learning and predictive analytics, with a focus on how these approaches can be applied to petroleum engineering challenges such as improving production efficiency, enhancing asset reliability, and optimizing mature oilfield operations. This portfolio showcases my learning journey, research interests, and commitment to developing data-driven solutions for a more efficient and sustainable energy industry.
