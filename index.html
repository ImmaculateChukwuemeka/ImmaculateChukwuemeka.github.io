My Projects (Artifacts)

AI-Assisted Predictive Maintenance Analysis

Objective: Explore how AI can analyze oil and gas data to identify patterns and support predictive-maintenance decisions.

Process: Used AI-assisted analysis to clean, explore, visualize, and interpret pipe thickness-loss data while evaluating data quality, potential integrity risks, and the role of human judgment.

Tools: ChatGPT (OpenAI GPT-5.5), Excel, AI-Assisted Data Analysis

Value Proposition: The analysis identified 487 of 1,000 assets as Critical, with Critical assets averaging 7.46 mm of thickness loss compared with 0.92 mm for Normal assets. A risk-based prioritization approach further identified 269 Critical assets for the most immediate inspection queue. These findings demonstrate how AI-assisted analytics can support maintenance prioritization while reinforcing the importance of data quality, petroleum engineering domain knowledge, field validation, and human judgment.

ML for Oil & Gas Production Forecasting

Objective: Explore an end-to-end machine-learning project for oil, water, and gas production forecasting while evaluating its alignment with recommended ML practices.

Process: Reviewed a published NETL case study, examining its preprocessing, model selection, validation, evaluation metrics, ethical safeguards, and deployment considerations.

Tools: Published NETL Case Study, Machine Learning Best Practices, APA Research and Citation

Value Proposition: The reviewed study found that its Proprietary LSTM Model achieved average R² values of approximately 0.86 for oil and 0.79 for both water and gas, highlighting the predictive value of richer operational and geological data. The analysis reinforced the importance of industry data access, realistic validation, data governance, and domain-specific model development when applying machine learning to oil and gas production forecasting.

Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning Analysis

Objective: Compare machine learning and deep learning techniques by evaluating their suitability for structured and unstructured real-world applications.

Process: Reviewed real-world applications, evaluated model-selection considerations, and used generative AI to create an infographic communicating the comparison visually.

Tools: Web Research, Case Study Analysis, Generative AI (ChatGPT), AI Image/Infographic Generation

Value Proposition: The analysis found that traditional machine learning is often more practical for structured data with known features, while deep learning is better suited to complex, unstructured data where automatic feature learning is valuable. The project demonstrates the ability to evaluate AI approaches based on the problem, data characteristics, interpretability needs, computational requirements, and business context.
